# Fibonacci logo generation

Created with the built-in Codex image generation tool, 22 October 2026. Option 2 was selected for its broad six-seed spiral and clear gold center. Options 1 and 3 are preserved as `logo-alt-1.png` and `logo-alt-2.png`; `logo-source.png` is the unmodified selected output.

Production PNGs preserve the generated silhouette and transparency, normalize the two colors to the studio tokens, and use Lanczos resizing. The dark header variant uses #8ea6ff and #f0bf5a. Apple icons have an opaque white background. The SVG progress glyph is intentionally unchanged. No vector trace was substituted for the generated artwork.
