# Public launch owner gates

The repository does not contain enough verified company or product facts to publish accurate legal promises. These decisions need owner and qualified legal/privacy review before public launch.

- **Privacy notice:** identify the legal operator and contact, countries served, data collected, purposes, providers/subprocessors, international transfers, retention periods, security practices, user rights, and deletion timelines.
- **Terms of service:** define eligibility, acceptable use, generated-content rights and limits, service availability, billing/refunds if applicable, and dispute/contact details.
- **Children and parental consent:** the product is designed for ages 10–18. Confirm target countries, applicable age thresholds, parent/guardian consent and notice requirements, age assurance, parental access/deletion processes, and whether the product should exclude younger users in any region.
- **Data minimization and retention:** decide retention for account profile data, generated scripts/videos, logs, backups, analytics, and failed jobs. Confirm whether any content is used to improve provider models and configure provider controls accordingly.
- **Safety operations:** assign a contact and response process for child-safety reports, abuse, security incidents, and urgent deletion requests.
- **Public policy pages:** after those facts are approved, publish the final privacy notice and terms at stable public URLs and link them from sign-up and the site footer.

Account deletion is implemented in the app and API, including user media cleanup. It does not supply the legal notice, determine consent requirements, purge provider-side billing/security records, or shorten backup retention automatically. Document those actual limits in the approved notice.
